**original_problem_statement:**
The user requested the development of a comprehensive mobile application for an Auto Repair Shop Management System.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (Based on initial request and subsequent user feedback):**
- **Roles & Permissions:**
    - **Admin:** Manages users (technicians), clients, orders, and system settings. Can view all financial and operational reports. Has full control over order statuses and pricing. Manages client credit accounts.
    - **Technician:** Can scan VINs, create/select clients, create work orders, and update order status in a forward progression. Can view their own assigned orders but **cannot** see financial data (prices, totals, financial reports). Can confirm/adjust the total price of an order assigned to them.
- **Main Workflow:**
    1.  **VIN Scan:** Scan a VIN to pre-fill vehicle data.
    2.  **Client Management:** Assign the vehicle to an existing client or create a new one. Admins can mark clients as having a Credit Account.
    3.  **Work Order Creation:** Create an order by selecting services from a checklist (without individual prices) and entering a single total price for the job.
    4.  **Order Assignment:** Admins assign orders to technicians.
    5.  **Price Confirmation:** Technicians can review and adjust the total price on an assigned order.
    6.  **Status & Comments:** Track order progress through states (Assigned, Initiated, Pending, Completed). Add comments to the order.
    7.  **Payment:** Record payment details. This option is hidden for technicians if the client has a Credit Account. Once an order is marked Paid, the status cannot be reverted.
- **Admin Features:**
    - **Daily Reports:** View summaries of cars serviced, total billed, paid, and pending amounts.
    - **Credit Reports:** A dedicated screen to view outstanding balances, grouped by client, with an option to mark individual orders as paid.
    - **User Management:** Admins create and manage all user accounts. Public registration is disabled.
- **UI/UX:**
    - The company's logo is on the login/splash screens.
    - A daily Bible verse is on the main dashboard.
    - Technicians' dashboards show only car counts, not monetary values.

**User's preferred language**: Spanish. The next agent MUST respond in Spanish.
