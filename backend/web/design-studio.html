How to Use

How to Create Your Menu

  1. Choose a size — Use 1920x1080 for landscape screens or 1080x1920 for portrait
  2. Add your content — Use Text, Photos, Shapes from the left panel
  3. Upload your food photos — Click "Uploads" in the left panel to add your own images
  4. Use templates — Click "Templates" to start from a pre-made design
  5. Customize — Change colors, fonts, sizes, positions
  6. Export — Click the download button (top right) to save as PNG or JPG
  7. Upload to MediAd View — Use the exported image as media content in your campaigns or as a menu background

This editor is 100% free. No account required. Your designs stay private in your browser.
