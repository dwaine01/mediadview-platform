About Us

MediAd View is an innovative digital display company offering a complete line of professional LED solutions for indoor, outdoor, and storefront window applications.

With installations nationwide, MediAd View displays are designed for environments with high ambient light. Brightness levels up to 5,500 nits allow our ultra-high-brightness LED displays to remain visible even in direct sunlight — making them ideal for storefront windows, churches, retail spaces, and outdoor walls.

We offer one of the industry's most complete portfolios of ultra-bright professional LED displays, backed by dedicated support and cloud-based content management solutions.
