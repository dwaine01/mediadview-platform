Meet The Founder

Josue Tejada

CEO & Founder — MediAd View LLC

MediAd View is an innovative digital display company that offers a complete line of professional LED solutions for interior, exterior and window applications.

With installations nationwide, MediAd View displays are the proven solution for environments with high ambient light. Brightness levels as high as 5,500 nits allow our ultra-high brightness LED to shine bright in direct sunlight — ideal for storefront windows, churches, and outdoor walls.

We offer the industry's biggest portfolio of ultra-bright professional LED displays, backed by dedicated support and cloud-based content management.
