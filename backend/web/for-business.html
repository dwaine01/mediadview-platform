Built to last

Infrastructure you can trust.

MediaView is built on cloud infrastructure designed for reliability — so your screens keep running even when things go wrong.

📡

Cloud-Managed Screens

Every screen is registered and managed from the cloud. Status, content version, and connectivity are all monitored in real time from your dashboard.

📶

Offline Resilience

If your internet connection drops, the player continues showing your last published content without interruption. Screens never go blank unexpectedly.

🔄

Automatic Recovery

The player automatically reconnects and syncs the latest content when connectivity is restored — no manual intervention required from your team.

🔒

Secure by Design

Each account is isolated. Role-based access means your staff can update content without touching billing or sensitive settings. Your data stays yours.

👥

Team Access Control

Invite staff members with scoped permissions. A manager can update the menu without accessing account billing. Full control, zero risk.

💳

Payments via Stripe

Subscription billing is processed securely through Stripe. Your payment information is never stored on our servers — Stripe handles it all.
